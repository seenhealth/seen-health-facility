# Olympic & Alveare — room review, 22 September 2026

This revision uses the supplied architectural PDFs as evidence for room boundaries, entrances and furniture symbols. It preserves the Seen equipment families, finishes, fleet and neighborhood models. It is a design visualization, not an as-built survey.

## 1630 Olympic

- First floor: memory-care enclosure, separate staff alcove and accessible toilet/shower, quiet room, group activities with operable folding wall, storage and toilet rooms, reception/vestibule, servery, kitchen/stores, electrical, receiving and veranda are modeled individually.
- Upper floor: longitudinal reception counter, four waiting seats, medication-room perimeter cabinets, continuous U-shaped nurses/providers workstation, 26 admin workstations, break-room east-wall casework and dining seats, exam-room door corners and diagonal examination chairs.
- PT: evaluation bays, hydro/linen area, treatment room, work area and equipment layout. Unspecified PT equipment is a proposed fit-out using the Seen catalog.
- Both full stair cores use opposing flights with a return landing. The existing elevator, its adjacent service shaft, and the new elevator remain distinct. Floor apertures are aligned with the same lower-floor core positions.
- The short stair above Toilet 4 ascends westward by **2 ft / 0.6096 m**. The raised western PT/shower/day wing remains part of Level 1; reception and the main day center remain at datum 0.
- Main and alternate A-2 clinic arrangements both receive the room and door review.

### Drawing questions that remain open

The 2-foot rise is user-confirmed. The precise raised-platform boundary is inferred from the stair; confirmation was requested. The drawing shows two full stair cores plus the short stair. A separate third full core has not been located in these sheets. Floor-to-floor height remains estimated at 3.8 m.

A-1 specifies the kitchen shell and support-room arrangement but does not show an appliance schedule. Its interior counters and appliances are proposed. The veranda outline is drawn, while its covered-entry design is explicitly TBD in the source.

## Alveare Terrace

The two latest attachments match the previously supplied PDFs byte-for-byte. They have been re-reviewed room by room rather than treated as new revisions.

- A2.01 supplies room boundaries and entrances, including the three non-food stores, quiet-room storage, janitor closet, booster-pump room and two distinct elevator cars.
- The user-supplied Mannigan **ID7.10** governs rooms 110 and 113: **eight** 60 × 30 inch desks, a **54-inch** divider, L-shaped break casework, refrigerator, dishwasher, sink, two stools, copier and separate office. Its furniture arrangement takes precedence over the different A9.06 arrangement.
- A9.05 supplies the day-room groupings; A9.07 supplies the reception counter, lobby seating, office and rehab mirror locations. QF101 supplies the commercial kitchen arrangement and beverage station.
- Two enclosed stairs use return flights. Open Stair 3 is represented as a straight ascending stair over the shared-access/mail area; its upper rise remains indicative.
- Rehab equipment and furniture not specified by the sheets are proposed Seen catalog items.

Local ramps, small floor-level transitions and external gradients still require detailed elevation coordination. Residential upper floors remain exterior massing.
