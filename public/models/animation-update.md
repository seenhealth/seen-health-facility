## Arrival through departure

## Shared timeline

One twelve-minute loop presents a representative 8 AM–4 PM day. The display compresses time; the shown durations are illustrative and do not establish actual appointment or travel times. It drives vehicles, doors, ramps, walking, clinical care, paired rehabilitation, tabletop OT, group movement, meals, social work, reception and upstairs coordination. Existing street traffic uses the same clock.

The timeline offers separate **People & vans** and **Interactions** views. It contains 41 person tracks, two vehicle tracks and 38 shared interaction tracks, filterable by workflow or searchable by name/role. Full-day, two-hour and thirty-minute horizons support closer inspection. Selecting a stage pauses at that stage and follows the person or shared interaction; selecting the track name follows from the current time. Play, pause, restart, speed, role filters, route overlays and figure-size controls apply to the scene.

## Source basis and limits

The read-only Orbit review covered three participant schedules and 30 bookings with linked orders and coordination comments. It informs clinical, therapy, social-work, escort, transport and coordination categories. The owner's updated description supplies the entrance and arrival sequence. All people, staffing, gestures and times are representative composites; no participant identities, record IDs, diagnoses, addresses or live records are included. No Orbit records were changed.

The three guided Participant journeys retain their separate recorded-duration narratives and unresolved source distinctions. The animated care day is a representative simulation. Inter-floor connections are not animated, and upstairs placement and neighboring heights remain estimated. The photographed upstairs fit-out and three-dimensional streets, sidewalks, landscaping, parking and neighboring massing remain in place. Plan, exploded and separated-level views hide moving people.

Native model and simulation checks cover continuity, wall clearance, ramp elevation, vehicle/boarding synchronization, automatic entrance opening, escort following, pause, seeking, loop repeat, speed, level visibility, stable template IDs and exported rigs/clips. They validate the authored scene, not real-world accessibility dimensions or clinical capacity.
